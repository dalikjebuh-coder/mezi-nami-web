How it works

01

Pick a topic and a level

Agree on what you want to talk about today. To start, try the “Warm-up” questions.

02

Let your kid pick a card

Read the question and take turns answering it. You can go first, so your kid sees that asking and listening go both ways.

03

High-five. And draw again

If you want to keep talking, flip the card for more questions or pick a new one. Five minutes is all it takes.

Before you start, agree on three things

So you both feel safe, even with the questions that aren't easy to answer.

„

Nobody gets in trouble for the truth.

We can say what we really think and how we feel.

„

We let each other finish.

We listen and don't interrupt.

„

Saying “stop” is enough.

If we don't want to talk about something, we skip the question or end the talk.

Questions and answers

What age is the app for?

The questions are written mainly for kids ages six to nine. They can work for younger or older kids too. It depends on what they're into and what they want to talk about. The parent answers as well.

Are our answers saved anywhere?

No. You answer out loud, and the app never saves or sends your answers. All that stays on your device is the names you entered and which questions you've already done.

How much does the app cost?

It's free, with no ads and no extra payments. One dad built it.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After the first launch you can use it without a connection.

Can we use it on Android?

Yes, just open it in your browser. You can add it to your home screen and use it offline too. A Google Play version is in the works.

Is the app a substitute for professional help?

No. The Two of Us helps you start talking. It doesn't replace psychological, therapeutic or medical care. Crisis lines and professional help are listed inside the app.
